## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to understand customer purchasing behaviour, evaluate sales performance, identify effective acquisition channels, and uncover opportunities to improve conversion rates and customer retention.

Using **Microsoft Excel, PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI**, raw e-commerce datasets were transformed into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, KPI development, and interactive dashboard creation.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from raw data preparation to business recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour and shopping patterns.
- Evaluate conversion rates across different funnel stages.
- Identify high-performing customer acquisition channels.
- Measure revenue, order volume, and average order value.
- Analyze product and category-level sales performance.
- Understand customer retention and repeat purchasing behaviour.
- Develop interactive dashboards for business reporting.
- Generate actionable recommendations for business improvement.

1. **Data Collection:** Imported five e-commerce datasets from Excel.

7. **Reporting:** Documented findings and translated them into business recommendations.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Conversion Funnel Analysis
- 1,157 customers viewed products.
- 730 customers added products to their carts.
- 531 customers initiated checkout.
- 390 customers completed purchases.
- Overall view-to-purchase conversion was 33.71%.

**Insight:** The largest proportional conversion loss occurred between product viewing and cart addition, highlighting an opportunity to improve product engagement.

### 2. Customer Acquisition Analysis

| Acquisition Channel | Customers | Revenue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Organic Search | 295 | ₹23,205.55 |
| Paid Search | 243 | ₹12,726.09 |
| Direct | 190 | ₹12,526.22 |
| Social Media | 199 | ₹12,145.85 |
| Email | 179 | ₹9,302.13 |
| Referral | 94 | ₹5,869.62 |

**Insight:** Organic Search generated the highest customer volume and revenue, making it the strongest acquisition channel in the analysis.

### 3. Product Performance
Electronics emerged as the highest revenue-generating product category.

### 4. Monthly Revenue Performance
Revenue demonstrated an upward trend from January to June 2025, with June recording the highest revenue of ₹37,602.41.

### 5. Customer Retention
- Retained Customers: 326
- Churned Customers: 64
- Retention Rate: 83.59%
- Churn Rate: 16.41%

**Insight:** The analysis identified opportunities to strengthen customer engagement and encourage repeat purchases.
